# User guide

## Citizens

**Create an account.** Choose Create an account, enter your name, email and a password of at least 8 characters, and keep the role set to Citizen.

**Find issues near you.** The home page lists issues within 5 km. Allow location access to see your own area. Use the tabs for Most supported, Latest and My reports, and the category menu to filter. On a phone, switch between List and Map.

**Report an issue.**
1. Select Report an issue.
2. Choose the category, write a short title and details.
3. Add a photo. If the app thinks it shows a different type of issue, it offers to switch the category. You decide.
4. Check the pin. It starts at your location; tap the map to move it. Add a ward or area so your local representative can see it.
5. Submit. If a similar report exists nearby, you will see it first. Choose Support this report to back it, or Report it anyway if yours is a different issue.

**Back, discuss and follow.** On any issue, "I face this too" adds your support, which moves it up the Most supported list. You can also like, repost and comment. The Updates page in the header shows status changes and replies on issues you reported or supported.

**Verify a fix.** When the municipal team uploads an after photo, the issue shows Fix awaiting verification. Open it and compare the before and after photos. Choose Yes, it is fixed to close it, or No, still a problem to send it back.

## Local representatives

1. Create an account and choose Local representative. Enter your ward or constituency.
2. Municipal staff must verify you before you can post. Until then you can read and comment. A banner tells you your status.
3. Open My portal.
   - **Complaints in your ward** lists the most supported issues. Use Open and reply to answer citizens (your comments carry a Verified representative badge). Use Escalate with a short reason to flag an urgent issue to the municipal office. You cannot change official status.
   - **Post an update** publishes news such as completed work, with optional before and after photos.
   - **My updates** lists what you posted and lets you delete it.
4. Citizens see your posts on the Development updates page, which can be filtered by ward.

## Municipal staff

Log in with the admin account. You land on the dashboard.

1. **Read the tiles.** They show total and open complaints, new ones not yet reviewed, fixes waiting for citizens, and average time to resolve.
2. **Pick a complaint** from the list. Filter by status or category, and sort by support or date.
3. **Review the evidence.** The panel shows the description, photos and map link. The AI assist box gives a summary, a suggested department, and warnings if the photo does not match the category or if repair evidence looks suspicious. These are hints; you decide.
4. **Move it forward** with the update form: Start review, Assign to a department, Mark in progress, then upload the after photo to request verification. Add a note for the public timeline if useful.
5. **Verify representatives** in the highlighted box at the top. Check official credentials before pressing Verify.

You cannot mark an issue Verified resolved yourself. That step belongs to citizens.

## Setting up the first admin
The admin account is created from `ADMIN_EMAIL` and `ADMIN_PASSWORD` when the backend first starts. Change the defaults before going live.
